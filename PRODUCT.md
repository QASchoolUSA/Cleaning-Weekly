# Product

## Register

brand

## Platform

web

## Users

Primary audience is people who need recurring cleaning for their home or small office — busy homeowners without time for deep cleaning, and office managers or small-business owners who want a dependable weekly service. They are usually comparing options online, skeptical of anonymous gig apps, and looking for something local they can trust long-term. Residential and commercial customers are both first-class; the booking flow should work for either without feeling like an afterthought.

Secondary audience is visitors who prefer talking to a person before committing — they may call instead of filling out a quote form on first visit.

## Product Purpose

Cleaning-weekly is a local cleaning service with a web presence that attracts new customers and lets them book recurring home or office cleanings. Success looks like visitors understanding the offer within seconds, trusting that the service is affordable and reliable, and either requesting a quote or calling to get started — then completing a booking flow that feels as polished as the landing page.

Both the marketing site and the booking experience carry equal weight from day one; neither should feel like a template tacked onto the other.

## Positioning

Affordable, simple, local recurring cleaning you can count on — not a gig-economy app, not a faceless national chain.

## Conversion & proof

- Primary CTA: Get a free quote / estimate
- Secondary CTA: Call or text us — for visitors who want to talk before committing
- The line a visitor remembers after 10 seconds: Your local weekly cleaning — affordable, reliable, booked in minutes.
- Belief ladder:
  1. This is recurring home or office cleaning, not a one-off task app.
  2. Pricing is transparent and affordable — no surge pricing or hidden fees.
  3. Cleaners are local, vetted, insured, and background-checked — not anonymous gig workers.
  4. Booking is simple and the same team shows up on schedule, every week.
  5. Request a quote or call — low friction, no corporate runaround.
- Proof on hand: Local trust signals — years in business, insured, background-checked cleaners. Add badges, certifications, and service-area details to `.impeccable/assets/proof/` as they become available.

## Brand Personality

Warm, trustworthy, and straightforward. The voice should feel like a capable local business owner who takes pride in their team — human and approachable, never corporate or salesy. Emotional goal: relief and confidence — "these people will show up, do good work, and not complicate my life."

## Anti-references

- Generic SaaS landing pages — purple gradients, stock handshakes, "Schedule a demo" energy, hero metrics, identical feature-card grids
- Gig-economy marketplaces — cluttered maps, anonymous workers, surge pricing, deal-hunter urgency
- Discount and coupon aesthetics — loud countdown timers, screaming yellow CTAs, coupon-code culture

## Design Principles

Practice what you preach on reliability — every surface, from quote form to confirmation, should feel dependable and finished; nothing half-built.

Trust before ask — surface local credentials, insurance, and vetting before pushing the quote form; earn belief in the ladder order.

Simple beats clever — booking and quoting should feel faster than calling a competitor; remove steps that don't build trust or move the job forward.

Warm, not corporate — copy and layout should read like a local business you'd recommend to a neighbor, not a Series B startup.

Landing and booking are one product — visual quality, tone, and interaction patterns should carry through from first visit to scheduled service.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Readable text, keyboard navigation, visible focus states, and meaningful labels on all forms. Honor `prefers-reduced-motion` on any animation. No formal WCAG audit yet; design and build to AA contrast and structure from the start so a later audit is straightforward.
