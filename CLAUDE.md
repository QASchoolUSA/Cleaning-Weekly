# Cleaning Weekly

Local recurring home and office cleaning service — web platform for attracting customers and booking cleanings.

## Design Context

Strategic context lives in **PRODUCT.md** (register: brand, platform: web). Visual direction lives in **DESIGN.md** (seed scaffold — re-run `/impeccable document` after implementation).

**Positioning:** Affordable, simple, local recurring cleaning — not gig-economy, not corporate chain.

**Primary CTA:** Get a free quote. **Secondary:** Call or text us.

**Design principles:** Trust before ask; simple beats clever; warm not corporate; landing and booking are one product.

**Visual north star:** "The Trusted Counter" — committed warm teal, display + mono typography, responsive motion, local-service-site references. Avoid generic SaaS slop.

For any UI work, read PRODUCT.md and DESIGN.md before generating screens.
